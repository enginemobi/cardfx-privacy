CardFX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

CardFX displays credit-card currency conversion rates published by card networks. It is free, contains no advertising, and has no in-app purchases.

Short version

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.
No account is required, and we do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind.

What the app does

CardFX sends your selected currency pair (for example, AUD to CNY) to the CardFX exchange-rate service, which returns the conversion rates published by card networks. Requests contain no personal information, no device identifiers, and no location data.

What is stored

The app stores a small cache of recently fetched rates and your last selected currency pair on your device only, so it can show rates offline. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

CardFX requests a single permission: network access, needed to fetch rates. It accesses no camera, contacts, location, storage, or other data on your device.

Children

CardFX is a utility for currency conversion and is not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address, and the "last updated" date above will change.
